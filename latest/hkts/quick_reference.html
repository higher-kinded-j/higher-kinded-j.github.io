Applicative, Selective or Monad? decides between Applicative, Selective and Monad. Behaviour belongs to the instance, not the type class: Either's ap stops at the first error, while Validated's joins them all with its Semigroup.

Each linked page, apart from MTL Capabilities, states its type class's laws, and the hierarchy shows which type class extends which, drawn from hkj-api itself.
